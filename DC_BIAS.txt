AC Coupling Recommendations
When receiving data from an AC coupled driver (like a clock source), 
care must be taken to ensure the appropriate bias levels are selected 
so that the receiver's input threshold requirements are met. 
If the receiver uses LVDS15 and resides in a 1.5V powered bank, 
a DC_BIAS value of DC_BIAS_1 along with DIFF_TERM_ADV setting of TERM_100 ensure 
that both a DC bias level and termination are provided inside the IOB for an AC coupled input.
